# Swam Pyae Paing

### AI Engineering · Data Analytics · Computational Social Science

I build and study data-driven systems, from applied AI workflows to quantitative research on cities, mobility and public policy.

I work as an **AI Training Specialist** and hold a bachelor's degree from **Masaryk University**. My work combines hands-on AI evaluation with research in large language models, network analytics, natural language processing and statistical modelling.

Currently developing **[PoliScope AI](https://github.com/swampyaepaing/poliscope-ai)**, an evidence-grounded approach to analysing European policy discourse.
