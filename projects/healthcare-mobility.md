# Healthcare Mobility Modelling in Czechia

**Collaborative research | Spatial interaction modelling | Gravity model**

## Overview
This project studies healthcare mobility and patient flows between geographical areas in the Czech Republic. A gravity modelling framework was explored to represent interactions between places using location, distance and population information.

## My contribution
I contributed to **development of the gravity model**. I do not claim ownership of the overall project, its underlying data collection, or all code in the collaborative notebook.

## Methods observed in the research notebook
- Aggregated interdistrict healthcare visits and geographic representation.
- Gravity-model estimation and predicted flow matrices.
- Python and PyTorch model implementations with optimization and comparisons of loss functions.
- Year-based modelling and exploratory evaluation.

## Availability and reproducibility
The original research notebook depends on external healthcare files and geographic datasets that are not included here. The notebook has not been publicly released, as the rights and privacy conditions for research data and collaborative code need to be established. This page is a project description, **not** a reproducible release or a claim of public data access.
