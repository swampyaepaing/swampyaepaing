# Herding Behaviour in US Banking Stocks (2014–2024)

**Bachelor's thesis research | Financial econometrics | Behavioural finance**

## Research objective
Investigate evidence of herding in US bank stock returns and how estimated return-dispersion relationships differ across financial stress and monetary-policy regimes.

## Research approach
The study used daily bank-stock returns, cross-sectional absolute deviation (CSAD), and regression specifications that considered event regimes. The documented research covered COVID-19, the March 2023 US regional banking turmoil, and the Federal Reserve tightening period.

## Interpretation and limitations
CSAD regressions offer a statistical operationalization of herding, not direct evidence of individual investors imitating one another. Interpretation depends on the stock sample, market proxy, time windows, model specification and treatment of uncertainty.
